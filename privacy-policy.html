Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 • Alpine Coat Gem, Manhattan Atelier

1. Introduction & Atelier Privacy Philosophy

At Alpine Coat Gem, operating our bespoke luxury outerwear showroom at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, we hold client privacy and personal confidentiality to the absolute highest standard of artisan discretion. We understand that ordering a custom tailored overcoat, reserving private fitting consultations, or ordering noble cashmere swatches requires you to share personal anatomical measurements and contact records. This comprehensive Privacy Policy details the exact protocols we employ to collect, safeguard, process, and retain your data across our digital platforms and flagship salon.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you engage with our bespoke atelier, we collect essential client information including your name, telephone number, email address, physical delivery addresses, and custom coat pattern measurements taken during your private fitting sessions. Additionally, when you utilize our interactive climate matcher quiz or browse our lookbooks, our servers record technical telemetry such as IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and referral paths through Google Tag manager. These technical data points help our digital engineers maintain site security and optimize interface responsiveness for international luxury patrons.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Data Processing

We process your personal information exclusively for lawful commercial purposes, including handcrafting bespoke outerwear patterns, scheduling showroom visits, shipping swatch boxes via insured couriers, processing secure transactions, and delivering concierge service updates. Furthermore, with your explicit consent, we may send private invitations to seasonal alpine trunk shows and new fabric releases from Biella, Italy. We never sell, rent, monetize, or publicly distribute your personal information, anatomical measurements, or private transaction records to any third-party brokers.

4. Data Storage & Cryptographic Protection Standards

All digital records, client measurement archives, and payment information entrusted to Alpine Coat Gem are stored on private, enterprise-grade cloud servers shielded by 256-bit AES encryption protocols and multi-factor authentication. Digital payment transactions are handled directly through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways, ensuring our atelier never retains raw credit card numbers on local hardware. We perform continuous vulnerability assessments and cybersecurity audits to guarantee that your private information remains completely protected from unauthorized external access.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Access Controls

Regardless of your country of residence, Alpine Coat Gem extends comprehensive data privacy rights aligned with global standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You retain the absolute legal right to inspect the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to measurement profiles, restrict processing activities, or demand the permanent deletion of your client records from our systems. To exercise these statutory privacy rights, simply submit a formal request to our administrative desk at any time.

6. Data Retention Protocols & Archival Integrity

We retain bespoke client anatomical measurements and paper pattern archives for as long as your client profile remains active, enabling seamless repeat commissions of tailored overcoats without necessitating repetitive measurement sessions. If you choose to close your account or request data expungement, all electronic records and paper pattern drafts are securely shredded and permanently deleted within thirty business days, subject only to mandatory statutory tax and accounting retention requirements mandated by United States commercial law.

7. Contacting Our Data Protection Officer

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to discuss our client confidentiality practices with our management team, please contact our administrative desk. Written correspondence should be addressed directly to Alpine Coat Gem, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically to our concierge staff by calling +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours. Our compliance team will examine your communication with professional care and respond within standard business timelines.
